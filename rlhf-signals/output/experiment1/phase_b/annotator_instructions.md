# Text Editing Task — Instructions for Participants

## What is this study about?

We are studying how people edit text generated by AI assistants. Your edits will help us understand the range of changes that different people make when improving AI-generated content.

## Your task

You will be shown a series of text samples. Each sample was generated by an AI assistant in response to a user's request. Your job is to **edit the text to improve it**. Make whatever changes you think are needed.

There is no single "correct" answer. Different people will make different edits, and that is expected. Trust your own judgment about what makes the text better.

## What kinds of text will you see?

The samples come from real interactions with AI assistants. They include:

- Software development assistance (code explanations, debugging notes, deployment instructions)
- Document drafting (summaries, technical descriptions, process documentation)
- Technical content (architecture notes, configuration guides, status updates)

Some samples may be short (a single sentence) and others may be several paragraphs. Some may include code snippets or technical terms you are not familiar with — edit what you can and leave unfamiliar technical content as-is if you are unsure.

## How to edit

You will see the AI-generated text in an editable text area. Read it, then make your edits directly in the text area. When you are satisfied with your changes, submit.

You may:

- Fix errors (factual, grammatical, logical)
- Rewrite unclear passages
- Reorganize content for better flow
- Remove unnecessary parts
- Add missing information if obvious
- Leave the text unchanged if you think it is already good
- Replace the entire text if you think it is fundamentally flawed

## What we measure

We record:

- The text before and after your edits (to compute the degree of change)
- The type of change (fix, rewrite, restructure, etc.)
- The time you spend on each sample

We do **not** evaluate whether your edit is "correct." We are interested in the natural variation of editing behavior across participants.

## What to avoid

- **Do not paraphrase for the sake of paraphrasing.** If the text already says what it means clearly, changing the wording without improving meaning is unnecessary.
- **Do not artificially shorten or lengthen text.** Your edits should serve the content, not hit a target length.
- **Do not skip reading the text.** Random or empty edits will be detected and the submission will be rejected.
- **Do not use external tools** (spell-checkers are fine, but do not use AI assistants to generate your edits).

## Time and compensation

- There are approximately 10 samples per session.
- Expected time per sample: 2-3 minutes. Some shorter samples may take less than a minute; longer ones may take up to 5 minutes.
- Expected total session time: 20-30 minutes.
- Compensation: you will be paid at Prolific's recommended rate for your region, calculated for the expected session duration. If the task takes significantly longer than estimated, please contact us.

## Quality and rejection policy

Submissions will be rejected only if:

- Attention check questions are answered incorrectly
- Edits are clearly random (e.g., keyboard mashing, copy-pasting unrelated text)
- The task is completed in an unrealistically short time (under 5 minutes for 10 samples)

We will **not** reject submissions based on the content or quality of your edits. There is no penalty for making small changes or large changes — both are valid approaches.

## Data use and privacy

Your edits will be used for academic research on AI text quality. No personally identifiable information is collected beyond your Prolific ID. The resulting dataset may be published in anonymized form. By completing this task, you consent to this use.
