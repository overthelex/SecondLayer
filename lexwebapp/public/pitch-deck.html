LLM Training Plan

We are training a legal model for Ukrainian law. Three steps from fine-tuning to high-accuracy production model.

1

Fine-Tune

Fine-tune on millions of court decisions. Domain-specific embeddings and LoRA adapters. Target: outperform GPT-4o on Ukrainian legal tasks.

Open-weight models (DeepSeek, Qwen) on cloud infrastructure with NVIDIA H100 GPUs.
2

Scale

Scale to larger corpus and jurisdiction-specific adapters. Continued pre-training on 100M+ decisions across 22 countries.

Multi-jurisdiction coverage. Expand training data beyond Ukraine to CEE and EU courts.
3

RLHF

Reinforcement learning from human feedback with practicing lawyers. Target: >95% preference, <0.2% hallucination rate.

Constitutional RLHF -- Ukraine's Constitution as reward signal. LegalTech LLM Constitution as open standard.
