# LEX — AI Legal Analytics for Ukraine

**The Problem**

Ukraine has over 60,000 lawyers, around 6,000 licensed attorneys, 5,000 law firms, and 10,000 corporate legal departments — and they're all stuck using tools from the 2000s. The biggest players — LIGA:ZAKON and ZakonOnline — are legacy databases with no AI, no semantic search, no analytics, and no API. A lawyer doing case research today spends 2 to 3 full business days manually digging through court records. That's insane in 2026.

**The Solution**

LEX is an AI-powered legal analytics platform. We do what takes lawyers days — in minutes. Our system searches court decisions, analyzes judge performance, verifies legal citations, monitors legislation changes, and generates defense strategies — all powered by AI.

Here's a real example: a client came to us with an 11-year-old debt enforcement case — UAH 11.9 million. Our AI analyzed 473 documents across multiple government registries and identified 9 defense strategies in 15 minutes. A human lawyer would have needed a week.

We have one of the largest legal databases in Ukraine — over 120 million records:

We're also the first and only platform in Ukraine with native MCP protocol support — that's 118 AI tools that plug directly into Claude, GPT, and other LLMs. This is the future standard for AI-tool integration, and we already support it.
